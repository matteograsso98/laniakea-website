Software-defined orchestration for satellite constellations

We build intelligent orchestration software that unlocks simpler, more efficient and more scalable network management for satellite constellations.

Get in touch

The problem

As satellite constellations become larger and orbit closer to Earth,
traditional management tools are inefficient and too specialised to scale.

Constellation dynamics ≈7.5 km/s

At LEO orbital speed the network topology changes continuously. Traditional approaches are ill-equipped for such extreme dynamics.

Network complexity +10³ satellite links

LEO coverage requires hundreds to thousands of inter-satellite links. Classical methods under-perform under these conditions.

Performance requirements 5G and beyond

Next-generation non-terrestrial networks target an order-of-magnitude gain over current performance requirements.

Our solution

An intelligent orchestration layer that predicts optimal decisions over time, instead of reacting to events after they have already happened.

  1. Continuous network monitoringThe state of the constellation, links, traffic, resources, is observed continuously and in a unified way.
  2. Prediction of future network statesThe system anticipates how the network will evolve, enabling proactive rather than corrective decisions.
  3. Optimal orchestration decisionsRe-routing when a link breaks, resource allocation to guarantee quality of service, and selective shutdown of resources to save energy are just a subset of possible use cases.

What makes it innovative

Our activity at Laniakea is centred on the research and development of proprietary predictive orchestration algorithms for networks in motion.

From reactive to predictive

Current tools react to network events. Our approach anticipates them, reducing the impact of failures and congestion before they occur.

Software-defined and hardware-agnostic

The orchestration layer is decoupled from the space segment and can run both on-ground and on-board.

Extendible to NTN standards

Designed to be compliant with 5G/6G non-terrestrial network protocols: scalable and flexible for real systems.

Beyond satellites

The same technology applies to any kind of moving network, including drones and airborne platforms.
